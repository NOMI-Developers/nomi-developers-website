River's Edge District

Our primary undertaking is a mixed-use neighborhood along the Kalamazoo River, built out of a handful of formerly industrial buildings. Four residential buildings — Harrison Circle, Walbridge Common, the Ignertia Building, and the Norbridge Building — sit alongside commercial space now home to Hurlers Cross Irish Pub, Green Door Distillery, and a small, growing roster of local businesses.
